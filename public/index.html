⚠️ Safety Protocol & Legal Release

Community Run Waiver & Road Safety Agreement

Cebu Marathon 2027 • Tacloban Community Progressive Long Run Leg (16K • 21K • 26K • 32K)

🚨 CRITICAL SAFETY NOTICE: OPEN PUBLIC HIGHWAYS

This is a community-driven training run, NOT a closed-course race. The roads, coastal bypass, and city thoroughfares remain FULLY OPEN TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC (cars, motorcycles, buses, trucks, and public utility vehicles) at all times. Your personal safety is your top priority.

🛡️ 1. Safety As Priority & Road Rules
  • Stay on Designated Road Shoulders: Always run along the outermost curb, sidewalk, or road shoulder. Never encroach into active vehicle travel lanes.
  • Face Oncoming Traffic: On road segments without sidewalks or outer dividers, runners must run on the left side facing oncoming vehicular traffic to maintain clear visual awareness of motorists.
  • High-Visibility & Pre-Dawn Running: Run starts are scheduled between 02:30 AM and 04:00 AM in low-light conditions. Runners are strongly urged to wear reflective apparel, headlamps, or flashing LED blinkers.
  • Intersections & Traffic Signals: Obey all traffic signals, pedestrian crossing laws, and club volunteer marshal directions. Always look both ways before crossing junctions.
  • Auditory Awareness: If listening to audio devices, keep the volume low or use bone-conduction / single earbud headsets so sirens, horns, traffic, and marshal instructions remain clearly audible.
⚖️ 2. Non-Liability & Assumption of Inherent Risk
  • Inherent Risks: You acknowledge that long-distance endurance running on public highways involves inherent risks including, but not limited to, vehicular collisions, road surface hazards, slips, falls, heat exhaustion, and dehydration.
  • Waiver & Release of Liability: In consideration of participating in this free training run, you hereby voluntarily assume all risks and release, discharge, and hold harmless the organizing volunteers, lead running clubs (Tacloban Bypass Running Club, Tacloban Grandstand Runners, TRC Running Club, Motion Runners, One Stride Tacloban, Alangalang Runners Club, Santa Fe Runners), Cebu Landmasters, and Cebu Marathon organizers from ANY AND ALL CLAIMS, LIABILITIES, ACCIDENTS, INJURIES, FATALITIES, OR PROPERTY LOSS occurring prior to, during, or after the run.
❤️ 3. Physical Fitness & Personal Responsibility
  • Medical Fitness: You affirm that you are physically fit, healthy, and adequately trained to undertake the progressive distance (16K, 21K, 26K, or 32K) you selected.
  • Self-Sufficiency & Hydration: While participating running clubs may provide community hydration stops, runners are encouraged to carry personal hydration vests, bottles, and fuel. If you experience dizziness, chest pain, or distress, stop running immediately and notify a companion or marshal.
🎫 4. Cebu Marathon 2027 Authenticity

You certify that you are registered for the Cebu Marathon 2027 and that your uploaded proof of registration is authentic and truthful.
